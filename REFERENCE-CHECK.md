## Cat performance correction

Revisited YouTube in Chromium on September 14. The opening played briefly before YouTube reported a playback error; `outputs/reference-playback.png` captures the cat facing the viewer while shooting to its right at approximately 28 seconds. The user's explicit direction also requires the face/front torso to remain visible for upward and sideways aim. Exact frame-by-frame timing and ear/tail motion could not be measured reliably from this playback.

Replaced the full profile/rear cat bodies with a front-facing layered performance. The head tilts less than five degrees, ears flick and respond to firing, and the tail sways with restrained shot follow-through. Gun direction remains independent, with both paws and the muzzle attached to the physical weapon pose. No rear or one-eyed profile cat frames remain. Enemy/boss direction is preserved. This matches the requested performance style; it is not a verified frame-exact reproduction of the reference.

The subsequent hand/gun correction anchors opposite shoulders to the torso, uses smaller guns with a folded fish-can tab, and foreshortens upward gunfire. Actual reported-angle and turning captures were visually reviewed; coordinate correctness alone was insufficient. This remains a stylized two-paw waist hold, not a claim of exact reference rigging.
